WOODELLO MELAMINE
Woodello Melamine offers a smooth, elegant, and long-lasting finish that enhances the natural beauty of
wood. It provides excellent surface hardness, superior stain resistance, and high clarity — perfect for
both furniture and interior woodwork.
Variants: Matt, Gloss, Sealer
Available Pack Sizes : 1L, 4L, 20L With Melamine Hardner
DIRECTIONS FOR USE
Surface Preparation:
Sand wood along the grain using Sandpaper No. 80, 120, 220 followed by 320. Remove dust and debris.
Fill cracks or dents with Woodello Wood Filler as needed.
Application:
Mix Woodello Melamine (Sealer/Matt/Gloss) base with hardener in a 9:1 ratio and dilute with 20-30% Woodello
Melamine Thinner. Stir thoroughly and strain before use. Apply 2-3 coats by brush, spray, or cloth, allowing 5-6
hours of drying time between coats. Lightly sand with Sandpaper No. 320 before applying the topcoat.
Drying time at 30°C: Surface dry in 1-2 hours and hard dry in 12 hours.

WOODELLO NC
SANDING SEALER
Enhances wood surfaces with smooth, even base preparation. Ideal for
solid wood, plywood, veneer, and MDF. Prepares wood for topcoats with
excellent adhesion and grain-filling properties.
Available Pack Sizes : 500ml, 1L, 4L, 20L.
DIRECTIONS FOR USE
Surface Preparation:
Repair dents and cracks. Ensure surface is free of
dust before application. Use 80, 120, 220 grit
sandpaper for solid wood/plywood and 220-320 grit
for veneer/MDF. For best results, use a quality wood
sander.
Application:
Dilute 1:1 with Woodello NC Thinner for spraying. Adjust
dilution as needed for cotton application. Cloth: 2-4 coats,
sand lightly after alternate coats, with 20-30 minutes drying
time. Spray: 2 coats with a 1-1½ hour interval. For porous
wood, apply undiluted. Do not over-dilute.

WOODELLO NC
LACQUER
Woodello NC Lacquer provides a premium, fast-drying, and clear finish for
wooden surfaces. Ideal for solid wood, plywood, veneer, and MDF - it enhances
natural grain and ensures lasting protection with a smooth, elegant sheen.
Available Pack Sizes : 500ml, 1L, 4L, 20L.
DIRECTIONS FOR USE
Surface Preparation: I
Ensure the surface is clean, dry, and properly
sanded. Use Sandpaper No. 80, 120 followed by 220
to remove dust, dirt or old coatings.
Application:
Stir well before use. For spraying, dilute 1:1 with NC Thinner.

coats, allowing 20-30 minutes of drying time between coats.
Sand lightly between coats with 1000-grit sandpaper for best
results. Drying time (at 30°C): Surface dry 15-20 minutes, hard
dry 10 hours.

WOODELLO 1K PU
Woodello 1K PU delivers a rich, smooth, glossy & matt finish ideal for both interior and exterior
wooden furniture. Designed for lasting protection and elegance, it enhances the natural beauty of
wood while providing a durable surface that resists wear and tear.
Variants : Matt & Gloss
Available Pack Sizes : 200ml, 500ml 1L, 4L, 20L.
DIRECTIONS FOR USE
Surface Preparation: Sand the surface with Emery Paper No. 80, 120, and 220, followed by 320/400 grit for smoothness. Wipe
off all dust and ensure the surface is clean and dry.
Application:
For a coloured appearance, apply Woodello Wood Stain evenly using a clean cloth and allow 3 hours of
drying time. Do not sand the stain layer. Apply 2-3 coats of Woodello 1K PU Gloss/Matt using a brush or
spray at 16-hour intervals. For brushing, dilute with 10-15% Woodello Thinner; for spraying, dilute with
20-25% Woodello Thinner. Sand lightly between coats for a perfect gloss or matt finish.

COLOR FINISH

PROTEKTOR

cushions, and upholstery. This protective layer repels spills, dirt, and liquids-keeping your fabric fresh and stain-free.
It's like insurance against everyday accidents, giving you a license to relax and live freely.
Available Pack Sizes : 100ml, 200ml, 300ml, 500ml, & 1L.
How To Use
24h F
1. Clean the Fabric

2. Shake & Spray
