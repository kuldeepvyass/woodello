HOW TO APPLY
SURFACE PREPARATION
SANDING & INSULATING:
Sand the wooden surface along the grain using sandpaper no. 180, followed by 320
or 400 grit to achieve a smooth surface. Wipe the surface to remove loose dust
particles.
DENT FILLING:
If the surface has dents or cracks, apply Woodello Wood Filler using a putty knife
over the affected area and level the surface properly.
STAINING:
For colouring, apply Woodello Wood Stains for Interiors or Exteriors using the
ragging method. Allow the stain to dry for 30–120 minutes before applying further
coats.

POST APPLICATION CARE
If coating material accidentally spills on tiles, glass, or metal surfaces, clean immediately with a clean damp cloth or mild cleaning solution before the
coating dries. If stains become difficult to remove, gently wipe using a suitable cleaning agent. During spraying or brushing, surrounding areas should
be properly masked or protected to prevent unwanted stains on adjacent wood, walls, or coated surfaces.

APPLICATION RECOMMENDATIONS
For best results, apply Woodello Water Based PU at temperatures between 15°C and 35°C with relative humidity below 80%. Ensure the wooden
surface is properly sanded using 320–400 grit sandpaper and cleaned before application. Stir well before use and strain if necessary. Apply thin and
uniform coats using spray, brush, or roller. Allow proper drying between coats and lightly sand between coats for smoother finishing. Avoid application
under extremely humid or cold conditions to ensure proper curing and film formation.
SAFETY INFORMATION
Refer to the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for detailed safety instructions while handling the product. Store the material in a cool, dry, and
well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight. Use protective equipment such as gloves, safety glasses, and face masks during application. Avoid
direct contact with eyes and skin; in case of contact, wash immediately with soap and plenty of water. Do not ingest the product. Keep out of
reach of children. Dispose of leftover material or containers responsibly through authorized recycling or waste management agencies.
