PRODUCT OVERVIEW

coating systems. It provides proper dilution, excellent flow, and smooth application while
enhancing the finish quality and performance of PU coatings on wooden surfaces.

HOW TO APPLY
SURFACE PREPARATION
SANDING & INSULATING:
Sand the wooden surface along the grain using sandpaper no. 180, followed by 320
or 400 grit to achieve a smooth surface. Wipe the surface to remove loose dust
particles.
DENT FILLING:
If the surface has dents or cracks, apply Woodello Wood Filler using a putty knife
over the affected area and level the surface properly.
STAINING:
For colouring, apply Woodello Wood Stains for Interiors or Exteriors using the
ragging method. Allow the stain to dry for 30–120 minutes before applying further
coats.

Note:
Use only Woodello PU Slow Drying Thinner for dilution of recommended Woodello PU coating systems. Woodello PU Thinner should be properly mixed with
the coating material in the specified dilution ratio before application. The thinner must not be contaminated with oil, varnish, water, or other local solvents, as
this may affect coating performance. Once the container is opened, it is recommended to consume the thinner as early as possible and keep the container
tightly closed after use to avoid solvent evaporation and contamination. The recoat period mentioned for coating systems is based on 30°C temperature and
75% relative humidity, which may vary depending on environmental conditions. Recommended spray air pressure: 2.2 – 2.8 kg/cm² for compatible spray
applications.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
POST APPLICATION CARE
If coating material spills on tiles or metal surfaces, clean immediately using Woodello NC Thinner and wipe with a clean cloth. During spraying or
brushing, surrounding areas should be properly masked to prevent stains on adjacent surfaces.

APPLICATION RECOMMENDATIONS
For best performance, use Woodello PU Slow Drying Thinner with compatible PU coating systems at temperatures between 20°C and 40°C and
relative humidity below 75%. Do not over-thin the coating material and always follow the recommended dilution ratio. Stir well and strain before use
to achieve smooth application and proper finish.
SAFETY INFORMATION
Refer to the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for detailed handling instructions. Store the product in a cool, well-ventilated area away from heat,
sparks, and open flames. Use protective equipment such as gloves, safety goggles, and masks during handling.
Avoid contact with eyes and skin; in case of contact, wash immediately with soap and plenty of water. In case of inhalation, move to fresh air and seek
medical attention if discomfort persists.
This product contains no added Lead, Mercury, or Chromium compounds and is flammable. Dispose of leftover material and empty containers
responsibly through authorized recycling or waste management agencies.
