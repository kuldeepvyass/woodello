HOW TO APPLY
SURFACE PREPARATION
SANDING & INSULATING:
Sand the wooden surface along the grain using sandpaper no. 180, followed by 320
or 400 grit to achieve a smooth surface. Wipe the surface to remove loose dust
particles.
DENT FILLING:
If the surface has dents or cracks, apply Woodello Wood Filler using a putty knife
over the affected area and level the surface properly.
STAINING:
For colouring, apply Woodello Wood Stains for Interiors or Exteriors using the
ragging method. Allow the stain to dry for 30–120 minutes before applying further
coats.

Note: Ensure the wooden surface is properly sanded and free from dust, oil, grease, or other contaminants before application. Woodello NC Sanding Sealer
should be applied as the base coat to seal the wood pores and improve adhesion of the top coat. Allow proper drying and light sanding before applying
Woodello NC Lacquer. Always stir well before use and dilute only with Woodello NC Thinner as recommended. Avoid excessive thinning and ensure
containers are tightly closed after use to prevent solvent evaporation and contamination.
ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
POST APPLICATION CARE
If coating material accidentally spills on tiles, metal, or glass surfaces, clean immediately using Woodello NC Thinner and wipe with a clean cloth. If
stains become difficult to remove, apply thinner to the affected area and gently wipe or scrape the surface. During spraying or brushing, surrounding
areas should be properly masked to avoid unwanted stains on adjacent wood, walls, or finished surfaces.

APPLICATION RECOMMENDATIONS
For best performance, apply Woodello NC Sanding Sealer and NC Lacquer at temperatures between 20°C to 40°C with relative humidity below 75%. Do
not over-thin the material and always use Woodello NC Thinner for dilution. Stir well and strain before application. Proper sanding between coats is
recommended for a smooth and uniform finish.
SAFETY INFORMATION
Refer to the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for detailed handling instructions. Store the product in a cool, well-ventilated area away from sources of
ignition or fire. Use protective equipment such as gloves, safety goggles, and masks during application. Avoid direct contact with eyes and skin; in case
of contact, wash immediately with soap and plenty of water. Do not inhale vapours. This product is flammable, and leftover paint or empty containers
should be disposed of responsibly through authorized recycling or waste management agencies.
