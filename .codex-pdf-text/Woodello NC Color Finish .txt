Premium Nitrocellulose Wood Coating
PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Woodello NC Color Finish is a premium nitrocellulose-based coating designed to deliver a smooth,
durable, and high-gloss finish on wooden and metal surfaces. Its fast-drying formula provides rich colour
depth, excellent clarity, and easy application, making it ideal for furniture, handicraft items, and
decorative surfaces.

HOW TO APPLY
SURFACE PREPARATION
SANDING & INSULATING:
Sand the wooden surface along the grain using sandpaper no. 180, followed by 320
or 400 grit to achieve a smooth surface. Wipe the surface to remove loose dust
particles.
DENT FILLING:
If the surface has dents or cracks, apply Woodello Wood Filler using a putty knife
over the affected area and level the surface properly.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
POST APPLICATION CARE
If coating material spills on tiles or metal surfaces, clean immediately using Woodello NC Thinner and wipe with a clean cloth. During spraying or
brushing, surrounding areas should be properly masked to prevent stains on adjacent surfaces.
PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS
Drying Time:
Surface Dry: Approx. 20–30 minutes
Hard Dry: Approx. 1 hour
Gloss Level:
Gloss Finish: Minimum 80% (at 60° Gloss Meter)
Flash Point:
Above 30°C
Stability of Thinned Material:
Use the prepared mixture within 8 hours.
Shelf Life:
Up to 3 years from date of manufacture when stored in tightly closed containers away from heat and direct sunlight.
APPLICATION RECOMMENDATIONS
For best results, apply Woodello NC Color Finish at temperatures between 20°C and 40°C and relative humidity below 75%. Do not over-thin the
material and always follow the recommended dilution ratio using Woodello NC Thinner. Stir well and strain before use.
SAFETY INFORMATION
Refer to the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for detailed handling instructions. Store the product in a cool, well-ventilated area away from heat,
sparks, and open flames. Use protective equipment such as gloves, safety goggles, and masks during application. Avoid contact with eyes and skin; in
case of contact, wash immediately with soap and plenty of water. In case of inhalation, move to fresh air and seek medical attention if discomfort
persists.
This product contains no added Lead, Mercury, or Chromium compounds and is flammable. Dispose of leftover paint and containers responsibly
through authorized waste management agencies.
