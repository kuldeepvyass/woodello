PRODUCT OVERVIEW

beauty of wood while providing a durable, glossy finish. It forms a tough protective layer that protects
wooden surfaces from moisture, stains, and everyday wear while maintaining the natural wood grain

HOW TO APPLY
SURFACE PREPARATION
SANDING & INSULATING:
Sand the wooden surface along the grain using sandpaper no. 180, followed by 320
or 400 grit to achieve a smooth surface. Wipe the surface to remove loose dust
particles.
DENT FILLING:
If the surface has dents or cracks, apply Woodello Wood Filler using a putty knife
over the affected area and level the surface properly.
STAINING:
For colouring, apply Woodello Wood Stains for Interiors or Exteriors using the
ragging method. Allow the stain to dry for 30–120 minutes before applying further
coats.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
POST APPLICATION CARE
If coating material accidentally spills on tiles or metal surfaces, clean immediately using Woodello Multi-Purpose Thinner and wipe with a clean cloth.
If stains become difficult to remove, apply thinner on the affected area and gently wipe or scrape the surface. During spraying or brushing,
surrounding areas should be properly masked or protected to avoid unwanted stains on adjacent wood, walls, or coated surfaces.

APPLICATION RECOMMENDATIONS
For best results, apply Woodello Clear Varnish at temperatures between 20°C and 40°C and with relative humidity below 75%. Do not over-thin the
material and always follow the recommended dilution ratio using Woodello Multi-Purpose Thinner. Stir well and strain before application. Select the
appropriate thinner percentage depending on climatic conditions. In high temperatures, using a slow-evaporation thinner helps achieve better
leveling and a smoother finish.
SAFETY INFORMATION
Refer to the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for detailed safety instructions while handling Woodello Clear Varnish. Store the product in a cool, well-
ventilated area away from ignition sources and open flames. Use protective equipment such as gloves, safety goggles, and face masks during
application. Avoid contact with eyes and skin; in case of contact, wash immediately with soap and plenty of water. If inhaled, move to fresh air and seek
medical attention if discomfort persists.
This product contains no added Lead, Mercury, or Chromium compounds and is flammable. Dispose of leftover paint and empty containers responsibly
through authorized recycling or waste management agencies.
